# 06. Audit Evidence Tracker

## Purpose

For this stage, I'm looking at what evidence Harrow & Lane LLP would need to demonstrate that its information security controls are working.

Having a security policy isn't enough on its own. The firm should also be able to show evidence that its controls are actually being followed.

I've created a simple tracker covering eight important evidence requests.

## Priority Findings

### 1. Access Control Evidence — High Priority

R01 and R04 involve the possibility of unauthorised access to sensitive information.

I would prioritise checking MFA settings and access permissions because these controls help protect confidential client documents.

**Recommended action:** Request MFA configuration evidence and recent access review records.

### 2. Backup and Recovery — High Priority

The firm depends on cloud services to store important information.

Without successful restoration test evidence, I couldn't confirm that its backup arrangements would work during an incident.

**Recommended action:** Request the most recent restoration test report and investigate any outstanding issues.

### 3. Supplier Assurance — High Priority

The firm uses external providers that may handle client information.

I would need to review their security documentation and contractual arrangements before deciding whether the risks are adequately managed.

**Recommended action:** Ask the Compliance Officer to obtain supplier security documentation.
